# v1 has no money

Despite the name, v1 Sweepstakes are played for bragging rights only: no stakes, no pot, no payouts, and the site never records or moves money. The original idea (Entrants pay in, Organiser keeps 50%, closest guesses share the rest, site funded by a fee or by interest on held funds) would very likely be regulated pool betting in the UK, needing a Gambling Commission operating licence, and holding funds for months would need FCA authorisation or a payments partner — while interest on typical pot sizes is negligible and would not accrue to us anyway.

## Considered Options

- **Platform holds and pays out money**: rejected for v1 — licensing, KYC/AML and a payments partner before launch.
- **Platform records agreed stakes and computes payouts, money settled off-platform**: rejected for v1 to stay clear of facilitating betting at all; revisit only with legal advice.
- **No money, bragging rights only**: chosen.

## Consequences

Avoid money language in the domain (bet, stake, pot); see `GLOSSARY.md`. Revenue, if any, would come from something other than the pot (e.g. a flat fee to the Organiser).
