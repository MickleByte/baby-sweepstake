# Baby Sweepstake

Friends and family guess facts about an expected baby (date of birth, weight, sex) for bragging rights; once the baby is born, the closest guesses are declared winners.

## Language

### People

**Organiser**:
The person who registers an expected baby with the site and manages its Sweepstake.
_Avoid_: Parent, host, owner, admin

**Entrant**:
A person who has made at least one Entry in a Sweepstake. Entrants identify themselves by name only and never log in.
_Avoid_: Guesser, player, guest, bettor

### The game

**Sweepstake**:
One guessing game for one expected baby, created by an Organiser and shared with friends and family by link.
_Avoid_: Pool, game, event, baby

**Sweepstake page**:
The public page for a Sweepstake, reached by its shareable link, showing Predictions before the Result and Winners after it.
_Avoid_: Baby page

**Due date**:
The date the baby is expected, provided by the Organiser when creating a Sweepstake.

**Category**:
One fact about the baby that can be guessed (date of birth, weight or sex). The Organiser chooses which Categories are enabled for their Sweepstake.

**Entry**:
One submission to a Sweepstake, containing a Prediction for at least one enabled Category.
_Avoid_: Bet, stake, ticket

**Prediction**:
A single guessed value for one Category within an Entry.
_Avoid_: Bet, pick

**Closing date**:
The date after which no more Entries are accepted; defaults to 7 days before the Due date.

### Outcome

**Result**:
The actual facts about the baby, recorded once by the Organiser after the birth. Recording the Result permanently closes the Sweepstake; it cannot be changed afterwards.

**Not disclosed**:
A Category the Organiser chose to withhold when recording the Result; it has no Winners.

**Winner**:
An Entry whose Prediction is closest to the Result in a Category. Date of birth and weight have a single Winner unless tied on distance; sex has a Winner for every correct Prediction.
